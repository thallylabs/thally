---
title: "Badge"
description: "Compact status and category labels with intentional hierarchy."
---

Badges support semantic variants as well as a broader color palette, four
sizes, icons, and rounded or pill shapes.

## Examples

<Badge color="lime">Stable</Badge>{" "}
<Badge color="amber" shape="pill">Beta</Badge>{" "}
<Badge color="red" icon="triangle-alert">Deprecated</Badge>{" "}
<Badge color="blue" size="lg">Enterprise</Badge>

```mdx
<Badge color="lime">Stable</Badge>
<Badge color="amber" shape="pill">Beta</Badge>
<Badge color="red" icon="triangle-alert">Deprecated</Badge>
```

Existing `variant="success|warning|danger|info"` badges remain supported.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `color` | `gray \| blue \| cyan \| green \| lime \| yellow \| amber \| orange \| red \| rose \| purple` | `gray` | Color treatment |
| `size` | `xs \| sm \| md \| lg` | `sm` | Badge density |
| `shape` | `rounded \| pill` | `rounded` | Corner treatment |
| `icon` | `string` | — | Optional leading icon |
| `stroke` | `boolean` | `false` | Uses an outline treatment |
| `disabled` | `boolean` | `false` | Reduces emphasis and interaction |
