Thally helps software teams keep product knowledge aligned with the product.
Use it to publish documentation from Git, trace how merged changes affect your
docs and other customer-facing content, and review evidence-backed updates
before they publish.

## Publish with confidence

Connect the product repositories Thally Track should watch. When a pull request
merges, Thally gathers the evidence, finds the affected content, and prepares
an update for your team to review. If the evidence does not support an update,
no change is a valid result.
